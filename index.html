Hi, I'm Nuara!



I'm a Georgetown graduate student in Learning, Design & Technology with a background in computer science and over a decade of hands-on teaching experience in Brazil. My journey spans every stage of learning: from teaching children their first English words to mentoring adult teachers on building sustainable online businesses. Along the way, I built and sold my own online course to 100+ students, blending pedagogical theory with practical technical execution. What drives my work? An obsession with helping others learn and designing effective learning experiences, from planning to execution.
