The Problem

During the pandemic, thousands of EFL teachers in Brazil moved their teaching online for the first time — but most had no idea how to market themselves or attract students without a physical school behind them. Talented teachers were struggling to be found.

The Solution

Show Up — Insta for Teachers was the course my mentor and I built to close that gap. Through structured online modules and live Q&As, we taught EFL teachers with little to no Instagram experience how to market themselves and turn followers into students.

Sold on Hotmart to a Brazilian audience from November 2020 to September 2021, the course reached over 130 students across four core modules, plus live sessions with guest specialists.

What I'd do differently

  • Focus less on mindset and more on hands-on, actionable practice.
  • Build better support during the self-paced modules to improve completion rates.
  • Collect structured feedback at the end of the course, not just informally.
  • Document each live session for future reference and reuse.
