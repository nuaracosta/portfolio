The Problem

In 2020, many EFL teachers in Brazil lost their jobs, and had to pivot into working online. I was one of these teachers and started using Instagram to spread the word to my friends that I was teaching private EFL classes. From then on, I was able to attract over 30 students and successfully make the transition from teaching at schools into my own teaching practice.

The Solution

Show Up - Insta for Teachers was the solution me and my mentor at the time found to help those teachers in need. Through online classes, we were able to help teachers with low or zero Instagram knowledge and market their product to their audiences, and therefore, bring students into their businesses.

The course was sold on Hotmart to a Brazilian audience, from November 2020 to September 2021. We managed over 130 students in that time, and it had online modules as well as live Q&A classes every month for anyone in the cohort who wished to take part.
